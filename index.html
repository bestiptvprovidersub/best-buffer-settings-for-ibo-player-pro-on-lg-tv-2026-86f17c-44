Best buffer settings for IBO Player Pro on LG TV 2026

✍️ Sophie Morgan · 📅 October 2026 · ⏱️ 3 min read · 🛡️ Verified Review

Set your IBO Player Pro buffer to 5,000 milliseconds (ms) and switch your LG TV’s DNS to Cloudflare 1.1.1.1. This stops buffering and stuttering during 4K streams.

Follow the steps below to apply the settings.

What you need

  • IBO Player Pro app installed on your LG TV (webOS)
  • A valid IPTV subscription (we recommend SubTV IP for anti‑freeze servers)
  • Stable internet connection (Wi‑Fi or Ethernet)
  • LG TV remote control

How to change buffer settings in IBO Player Pro

  1. Press the Home button on your LG remote. Open IBO Player Pro.
  2. Press the Menu or Gear button on your remote to open the app’s side menu.
  3. Select Settings (the gear icon).
  4. Select Player Settings.
  5. Find Buffer Size or Cache Size (the exact name depends on the app version).
  6. Change the value to 5000 (milliseconds).
  7. Press OK or Back to save.
  8. Return to the main screen and restart the stream.

How to change DNS on LG TV to Cloudflare

Using Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) helps prevent your ISP from slowing down IPTV traffic. Follow these steps on your LG TV:

  1. Press the Settings button on your LG remote (cog icon).
  2. Go to Network > Wi‑Fi Connection (or Wired Connection if using Ethernet).
  3. Select your current network, then choose Advanced Settings.
  4. Change DNS Server from Automatic to Manual.
  5. Enter 1.1.1.1 as the primary DNS and 1.0.0.1 as the secondary.
  6. Press OK or Connect. Wait for the TV to reconnect.

If a step fails

  • Menu not visible? Press the Back button twice and try again.
  • Options greyed out? Make sure IBO Player Pro is fully updated. Check the LG Content Store for updates.
  • Buffering still happens? Restart your router and TV. Also test your internet speed – for 4K streams you need at least 25 Mbps download.

FAQ

Why does IBO Player Pro keep buffering on my LG TV?

Buffering usually happens because the buffer size is too small (default is often 1,000 ms) or your ISP is throttling IPTV. Set buffer to 5,000 ms and use Cloudflare DNS as shown above.

What is the best buffer time for IPTV in 2026?

5,000 ms (5 seconds) is the widely recommended value. It balances smooth playback with minimal delay. Higher values (e.g., 10,000 ms) may cause slower channel switching.

Does changing DNS affect other apps on my LG TV?

Yes, but it only improves network speeds for all apps. Cloudflare DNS is safe and often faster than your ISP’s default DNS.

How do I change DNS on an LG TV without a wired connection?

The steps are the same for Wi‑Fi. Go to Settings > Network > Wi‑Fi Connection > Advanced Settings and manually enter the DNS.

Is 5000 ms buffer safe for my TV memory?

Yes. IBO Player Pro manages memory automatically. 5,000 ms is a standard value used by many premium IPTV apps.

Can I use IBO Player Pro on other devices like Fire TV or Android TV?

Yes. IBO Player Pro works on Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Android TV boxes, and more. You can apply the same buffer settings on those devices.

What internet speed do I need for 4K IPTV without buffering?

At least 25 Mbps download speed. A wired Ethernet connection is more stable than Wi‑Fi for 4K streams.
